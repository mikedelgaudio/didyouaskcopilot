Did You Ask Copilot?

A short guide to asking your question the fast way, for both of you.

The slow way

Sam
hey, got a sec?
You
sure, what's up?
Sam
quick question about the auth service
You
ok, go ahead
Sam
why does this token expire after 5 minutes instead of the config value?
You
did you check if the env var is actually being read?
Sam
oh. let me look.
Sam
yeah that was it, thanks

The fast way

You
Copilot: why does my JWT expire after 5 minutes when TOKEN_TTL is set to 3600 in .env?
Copilot
Check whether the process is actually loading that .env file — a common cause is the config being read before dotenv runs, or a hardcoded default overriding it. Search for "expiresIn" in your codebase to confirm where the value comes from.
You
found it, thanks

Ask Copilot. Then ask your teammate — with the answer, or a much better question.

Why this matters

Every "quick question" costs your teammate a context switch, not just the minute it takes to answer. Most questions — what does this error mean, how do I use this API, why is this test failing, what's the syntax for X — are things an LLM answers instantly, with no one else's focus interrupted.

That doesn't mean never ask a human. It means ask the robot first, so that when you do ask a person, it's because:

  • Copilot got it wrong, or gave you something that didn't apply
  • The answer depends on context only a teammate has — a decision, a tradeoff, tribal knowledge
  • You need a second opinion or a sanity check on something you already tried

That's a much better question to receive — and a much better use of both your time.

This isn't "never ask a human." It's "don't make a human be your search engine." If you've genuinely tried and you're still stuck, or the question is about a decision rather than a fact, go ahead and ask — that's what teammates are for.
